What a slab leak is and why it hides

A slab leak is a leak in a pressurized water line that runs in or under a concrete slab floor. Most Seminole homes sit on slabs, so supply lines often run through or beneath the concrete. When a line corrodes, shifts or is abraded, water escapes under the slab, then finds a path upward through cracks, joints or walls.

Because the pipe is out of sight, the leak runs unnoticed until it surfaces. Meanwhile water saturates the soil, lifts flooring and wets wall bases.

Signs of a slab leak: warm floor spots, a running meter and the sound of water

  • A warm or hot patch on the floor, which can mean a hot water line is leaking beneath.
  • The sound of running water when every fixture is off.
  • A water meter that keeps moving when nothing is running.
  • A sudden jump in the water bill.
  • Damp carpet, lifted tiles, cracked grout or a musty smell near one spot.
  • Low water pressure at several fixtures.

To test, shut off every fixture and watch the meter's leak indicator. If it moves, close the main valve and see whether it stops. If the movement stops, the leak is on the house side. Then call a licensed plumber to locate and repair the line, and call (727) 626-0985 for the water cleanup. The emergency line is answered 24/7.

How the water damage is handled

  1. Shut off and stabilize

    The main valve stays closed until the plumber has repaired or rerouted the line.

  2. Extract surface water

    Water pooled on the floor is extracted, and wet carpet and pad are lifted.

  3. Dry the slab and walls

    Air movers and dehumidifiers dry flooring, wall bases and cavities. Concrete is slow to release moisture, so readings are tracked closely.

  4. Repair

    Flooring, baseboards and drywall are replaced when the structure reaches dry targets.

Why slab leaks appear in Pinellas homes

Copper lines under slabs can corrode over the decades, and soil movement, sandy Florida ground and water chemistry all contribute. Homes from the 1970s through the 1990s that still have original copper are most likely to see this. Repair choices range from spot repair to rerouting the line through walls or the attic, and a plumber decides which fits.

The ground under many Seminole streets near Park Boulevard is sandy and holds water, which means a slab leak can wash soil out from under the floor over time. That is another reason not to ignore a running meter.

Cost factors and insurance

The cleanup price depends on how much flooring is affected, whether tile or wood must come up, and how many drying days follow. Insurance often treats the sudden and accidental release of water as a covered event, but may exclude the cost of reaching and repairing the pipe, and often excludes ongoing seepage.

Policy wording varies, so read yours or ask your insurer. Our water damage claim steps guide covers documentation.

The ANSI/IICRC S500 standard covers the drying of concrete and framing and the monitoring of readings, which is why slab jobs record moisture daily.

Safety steps and when to call a plumber right away

  • Close the main valve as soon as you suspect a slab leak.
  • Do not use electrical equipment on a wet floor.
  • Be careful of hot water, since a leaking hot line can scald.
  • Call a licensed plumber to locate the leak with detection equipment.
  • Call the utility for any gas smell.
