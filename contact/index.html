What to tell the dispatcher

  • Your address and the nearest cross street.
  • Where the water came from and whether it has been stopped.
  • Which rooms are wet and how deep the water is.
  • How long ago it started.
  • Whether it is a home, a condo or a business.
  • Whether the water might be sewage or flood water.
  • Whether your insurer has been notified.

What to do while you wait

  1. Shut off the water at the main valve if you can reach it safely.
  2. Keep people and pets away from standing water near power.
  3. Lift valuables and small furniture off wet floors.
  4. Photograph the damage before moving anything.
  5. Do not run a household vacuum on wet carpet.
