Frozen pipes in a warm climate

Seminole does not see hard freezes every winter, which is exactly why pipes here are poorly protected. Homes built for Florida often run supply lines through unheated attics, garages and exterior walls with little insulation. When a cold front drops temperatures below freezing for several hours, those pipes can freeze and split, and the real damage arrives when the ice melts and water flows through the crack.

Hose bibs, irrigation backflow assemblies, pool equipment lines and water heater connections in garages are the usual failure points. The water often appears the next morning, after the temperature rises.

Emergency steps when a pipe has frozen or split

  1. Shut off the main water valve before the pipe thaws, if it is not already open and flowing.
  2. Open the affected faucet so melting water and pressure can escape.
  3. Do not use an open flame or a torch on a pipe. A hair dryer on low heat is safer for a short exposed section.
  4. If water is already flowing, close the main, stay away from wet ceilings and call (727) 626-0985.
  5. Photograph the frozen section and any damage before anything is moved.

Our emergency line is answered 24/7, and same-day response is available when a crew is free. If you are unsure whether a pipe has split, call anyway and describe what you see.

What damage a thawing pipe leaves

A split pipe releases water at supply pressure once it thaws. Attic pipes drop water through insulation and ceilings. Garage pipes flood slabs and drain across to interior floors. Because the break may be on a section nobody can see, the first sign is often a stain, a drip or a lower water pressure.

How restoration proceeds

  1. Stop and assess

    The water is shut off, and the extent of the leak is mapped with a moisture meter and camera.

  2. Remove standing water

    Extraction pulls water off floors and out of carpet. Insulation that is soaked is usually removed.

  3. Dry

    Air movers and dehumidifiers run, and readings are logged each visit.

  4. Repair

    Drywall, paint and flooring are repaired once the structure meets dry targets.

Preventing a repeat in a Florida house

  • Wrap exposed pipes in garages and attics with foam sleeves before a forecast freeze.
  • Disconnect hoses and cover hose bibs and irrigation backflow devices.
  • Know where the main shutoff is and test it each year.
  • On freeze nights, open cabinet doors under sinks on exterior walls.
  • If you travel in winter, ask someone to check the house after a cold night.

The National Weather Service in Tampa Bay issues freeze warnings when temperatures are expected to fall low enough to threaten plants and exposed plumbing. Watch for them, because a few hours below freezing is enough.

Cost factors and insurance

The cost depends on how long water flowed after the thaw, how many rooms were reached, how much insulation and drywall is wet and how much repair follows. Insurance often treats a sudden freeze-related break as a covered event, but policies differ on deductibles, exclusions and whether the home was left vacant.

Check your policy or ask your insurer, and read our guide on the steps of a water damage insurance claim.
