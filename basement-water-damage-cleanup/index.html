Basements in a high water table county

Pinellas County sits on a peninsula with a high water table, which is why full basements are rare. The below-grade spaces that do exist, including sunken living rooms, lower levels in split-level homes, stairwell landings and below-grade garages, are exposed in a way that most Seminole homes are not. Water arrives from rain, a rising table, a failed sump pump or a plumbing line, and it has nowhere to drain.

Because these spaces are cut into the ground, water that enters stays. Walls hold moisture from the soil side, and the floor is concrete that dries slowly.

Emergency steps for a flooded lower level

  1. Do not enter the space if water is deep or near electrical equipment. Switch off power at the panel only if it is outside the wet area and dry.
  2. Stop the source if it is a pipe, and check that the sump pump, if you have one, is running.
  3. Move anything valuable up and out, and keep the stairs clear.
  4. Photograph the water level and high-water marks.
  5. Call (727) 626-0985 and tell the dispatcher how deep the water is.

The line is answered 24/7, and same-day response is available when a crew is free.

Pumping, cleaning and drying

  1. Pump out gradually

    Deep water is removed in stages so pressure does not stress the walls and floor.

  2. Remove wet material

    Carpet, pad, cardboard, wet drywall and insulation are removed, especially if the water was ground water.

  3. Clean and sanitize

    Surfaces touched by ground water are cleaned and treated.

  4. Dry thoroughly

    Dehumidifiers and air movers run, with extra attention to wall bases and the slab.

  5. Prevent a repeat

    Gutters, grading and sump pump function are reviewed with the appropriate contractor.

Ground water brings soil and contaminants, so the cleanup follows the Category 2 to 3 approach on our flood damage page. Our water extraction page covers the pumping stage.

Mold risk in lower rooms

Cool, damp, poorly ventilated rooms support mold, and sunken spaces are slow to dry. The EPA notes that moisture control is the key to mold control, so the first job is thorough drying. If staining or odor persists, see our mold remediation page and the blog on how to clean mold off walls.

Seminole details

The ground near Lake Seminole and Boca Ciega Bay is low and wet, and heavy summer storms can push water into any space below street level. If you own a split-level home or a house with a sunken room, learn how water leaves your lot, and check how downspouts and the driveway slope near the house.

Cost factors and insurance

Cost depends on the depth and volume of water, the material removed, the cleaning scope and drying time. Ground water and surface flooding are often excluded from standard homeowners policies, while a plumbing failure may be covered, so check your policy and ask your insurer.
