What a wet ceiling is telling you

Drywall ceilings absorb water, then bulge, stain and eventually fall. A brown ring usually means water has been there a while, while a dripping, swollen patch means active flow. Behind the stain there is almost always wet insulation, which holds water against the drywall long after the source stops.

Common sources include roof leaks, AC condensate overflow, bathroom supply lines above a ceiling, water heater leaks in an upper closet and burst pipes in the attic. Finding the source matters before any repair, or the new drywall stains too.

Emergency steps for a sagging or dripping ceiling

  1. Keep people out of the room beneath the sag. A wet ceiling can collapse suddenly.
  2. Place a bucket under drips and move furniture and electronics away from the wet area.
  3. If water drips near a light fixture or fan, switch off power at the panel only if it is dry and reachable.
  4. Shut off the main water if a plumbing line is the likely source.
  5. Photograph the stain, then call (727) 626-0985.

A bulging ceiling can sometimes be relieved by a small controlled opening that drains trapped water into a bucket. That is better done by a crew than by a homeowner standing under it. The line is answered 24/7, and same-day response is available when a crew is free.

How ceiling restoration is done

  1. Find the moisture

    A moisture meter and thermal camera map the wet area, which is almost always larger than the stain.

  2. Open and drain

    Wet or sagging drywall is cut out. Soaked insulation is removed so the cavity can dry.

  3. Dry

    Air movers and dehumidifiers run until readings reach dry targets.

  4. Repair

    New drywall is hung, taped, textured and painted to match the surrounding ceiling.

Ceiling work often overlaps with wall and drywall repair and with structural drying.

Why Florida attics make ceiling leaks worse

Summer attics in Pinellas County get very hot and humid, and insulation traps moisture. When a leak soaks insulation, the heat and humidity above keep it wet for days. Seminole homes with low-slope or older roofs along Seminole Boulevard and the older streets near Park Boulevard are especially likely to hide a slow leak that shows up first as a ceiling stain.

The EPA says mold can grow on wet materials within 24 to 48 hours, which is why a wet ceiling should be opened and dried instead of painted over. Painting a stain only hides it. Our mold remediation page explains what follows if growth is already present.

Cost factors and insurance

Ceiling repair cost depends on the square footage of drywall removed, whether insulation is replaced, the texture match, and how much drying equipment runs. Roof leaks may involve a roofer as well. Insurance usually looks at the cause.

Sudden events such as a burst pipe are more often covered than gradual seepage, but your policy and deductible control the answer. Our guide to filing a claim for a small leak lays out how to weigh that.
