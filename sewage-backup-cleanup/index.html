Signs of a sewage backup: slow drains, gurgling and dark water in low fixtures

  • Water or waste coming up in a tub, shower or floor drain when you flush or run the washer.
  • Gurgling from toilets and drains, which suggests air pushed back by a blockage.
  • Several fixtures draining slowly at once.
  • A sewer smell in a garage, laundry room or bathroom.
  • Wet, dark staining on lower walls near a cleanout or floor drain.

One slow sink usually means a local clog. When several fixtures act up together, the main line may be blocked, and a plumber should clear or camera the line. Stop using water in the house until it is cleared.

Emergency sewage cleanup: what to do and what to avoid

  1. Stop using sinks, toilets, showers and washers so the backup does not worsen.
  2. Keep children, pets and anyone with a weak immune system out of the area.
  3. Do not walk through the water barefoot and do not try to wipe it up with household cleaners and a mop.
  4. Switch off power to affected areas only if the panel is dry and easy to reach.
  5. Call a plumber to clear the line, and call (727) 626-0985 for the cleanup. The line is answered 24/7.

Same-day response is available when a crew is free. The dispatcher will give you the real schedule on the call.

Why sewage is handled differently

The ANSI/IICRC S500 standard classes sewage as Category 3, meaning grossly contaminated. It can carry bacteria, viruses and parasites. The CDC advises people to avoid contact with sewage-contaminated water and to wash hands thoroughly after any exposure. For that reason, absorbent materials that touched it, such as carpet, pad, insulation and drywall below the wet line, are usually removed and disposed of properly instead of cleaned.

  1. Contain and protect

    Crews wear gloves, boots, eye protection and respiratory protection. The area is separated from clean rooms.

  2. Extract

    Contaminated water is pumped out and disposed of properly.

  3. Remove porous materials

    Carpet, pad, wet drywall and insulation are cut out and bagged.

  4. Clean and disinfect

    Hard surfaces and framing are cleaned and treated with an appropriate disinfectant.

  5. Dry and verify

    Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the structure, and readings confirm the result.

Sewer line problems in Seminole

Pinellas County has many older clay and cast-iron sewer laterals, and tree roots can enter joints. After heavy summer rain, municipal sewers and private laterals can also surcharge. A blockage in the main lateral outside your property is often the city's or county's responsibility, while a blockage in the private lateral belongs to the homeowner. Ask your utility which part of the line applies to you before arranging a plumber.

Homes in Oakhurst, Orangewood and the older streets off Park Boulevard sit on laterals that have been in the ground for decades. A camera inspection after any backup helps a plumber see whether a repair is needed.

Cost factors and insurance

Cost depends on the affected area, the volume of contaminated material to remove, the disinfection scope and drying time. Insurers often cover a sudden backup only if you carry an endorsement for sewer or drain backup, so look for that wording in your policy.
