Water damage restoration in Bardmoor

Bardmoor is a golf and tennis community in ZIP code 33777, centered on the Bardmoor Golf and Tennis Club on Cumberland Road between Lake Seminole and Belcher Road. The course opened in 1974, and golf-community sources say most of the housing was built in the 1970s and 1980s.

Dispatch runs from our Park Boulevard North office around the clock, and same-day response is available when a crew is free. Call (727) 626-0985 with the address, the source of the water and the rooms affected. Shutting off the main valve helps if it is within safe reach.

A mix of houses, condos and villas

Bardmoor is a group of subdivisions rather than a single development. Single-family neighborhoods include Bardmoor Estates, Bardmoor Golf View Estate and Golfwoods at Bardmoor. Condo and villa communities include Chateaux de Bardmoor, built between 1969 and 1979, The Cloisters at Bardmoor from the mid 1980s and Boulevard Club from 1978.

That mix means the first question is always what kind of building the water is in. A burst line in a house is a single-owner problem. In a condo, the same leak can wet the unit below, the hallway and the walls shared with neighbors.

Condos, associations and who handles what

Florida condominiums are run by associations, and responsibility for repairs usually splits between the association and the unit owner according to the declaration. The association may carry a master policy, and unit owners often carry their own. Read your declaration and policy, report the loss to the association and photograph the damage and source before cleanup.

Flood zones and storms

This page makes no claim about neighborhood-specific FEMA flood zones, because they are not verified. Enter your address in the FEMA Flood Map Service Center, or use the Pinellas County lookup, to see your own parcel. Hurricane season runs from June to November. If a storm brings water in from outside, it is usually Category 3 under the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard, and porous materials normally come out.

Drying and mold

Wet drywall, pad and insulation left in Florida humidity can support mold growth within a couple of days. The crew places dehumidifiers and air movers, logs moisture readings and keeps drying until walls and floors reach dry standards. Read about structural drying and mold remediation for the details.
