Water damage restoration in Seminole Lake Country Club

Seminole Lake Country Club is a golf community in ZIP code 33777. One golf-community guide counts about 200 homes in the deed-restricted Seminole Lake Country Club Estates, built from the mid 1960s into the 1980s. The grounds are shared with the neighboring Seminole on the Green, and townhomes and condos sit alongside the single-family streets.

We answer the emergency line 24/7 from the Park Boulevard North office. A crew can usually be sent the same day when one is free. When water is on the floor, call (727) 626-0985. Describe the source and the wet rooms, and turn off the water at the main if you can.

Why bigger homes mean bigger drying jobs

Many homes here are large. Sale listings in the community show floor plans from about 1,700 square feet to well over 3,000, and some homes are split-level or have pools. More floor area means more wall cavities, more flooring and more equipment on the job.

A leak that starts on an upper floor or in an attic air handler can travel through ceilings, walls and into the rooms below. Moisture mapping follows that path, so the crew dries the places the water actually reached. See our ceiling water damage and AC leak pages.

Associations and shared walls

The community has deed restrictions and homeowner associations, and the townhomes and condos have their own associations. In a shared building, a leak in one unit often reaches the unit below or beside it. The owner, the tenant and the association can each have a role, and the building may have a master policy with its own rules.

Tell the dispatcher whether the building is managed and who holds the keys. Photograph the damage and the source before cleanup, and read your association documents and your policy to see who handles what. Our guide on water damage claim steps outlines the documentation.

Flood zones vary by lot

Public listings for homes in this community show different flood designations on different lots, so a street-wide assumption is unsafe. Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status is not verified, and one lot's zone says little about the next. The FEMA Flood Map Service Center and the Pinellas County lookup both work by address.

Drying, mold and verification

Warm humid air slows drying and can support mold growth on wet drywall and pad within a couple of days. Our structural drying page explains how equipment is placed and how readings are logged until walls and floors reach dry standards. If growth has already started, see mold remediation.

Finding the community

From Park Boulevard, the community is entered by heading west toward Seminole, turning onto Seminole Boulevard and then onto Burning Tree Drive. Other streets include Greenbrier Drive, Tequesta Drive and Hershey Lane. Neighboring Bardmoor shares the 33777 ZIP code, and the service areas page lists every area we work.
