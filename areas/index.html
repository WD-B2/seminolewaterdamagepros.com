Seminole, Florida

Our home base is Seminole, a Pinellas County city of about 19,000 people built around Park Boulevard (State Road 694) and Seminole Boulevard. ZIP codes 33772, 33775, 33776 and 33777 cover the city and its edges. Neighborhoods include Oakhurst, Orangewood, Seminole Lake Country Club Estates, Lake Pearl Estates, The Gardens, Seminole Forest, Seminole Ridge and Parkwood.

Lake Seminole, Lake Seminole Park, Seminole City Park, Orange Blossom Park and the Fred Marquis Pinellas Trail run through the area.

Typical Seminole water damage involves slab homes with carpet or tile, attic and closet air handlers, garage water heaters and summer storm rain. See our water extraction, AC leak and storm water damage pages.

Indian Rocks Beach

A barrier island city on the Gulf with condos, canal-front houses and storm surge risk. Salt air and humidity change the plumbing and the drying. Read the page for water damage restoration in Indian Rocks Beach, which covers ZIP codes 33785 and 33786.

Saint Petersburg

The largest city in Pinellas County, with historic bungalows, mid-century slab houses, downtown condos and low bayfront blocks. Raised floors and plaster walls call for specific methods. Read the page for water damage restoration in Saint Petersburg, which covers ZIP codes 33701 through 33706.

Flood zones and storm risk differ widely across the county. The FEMA Flood Map Service Center shows the zone for any address, and Pinellas County publishes evacuation zones. If your policy has a named-storm deductible or excludes flood, read it before the season starts.

Flood and sewage-contaminated water are Category 3 under the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard, and that is true in every town we serve.

What to do before you call from any area

  • Shut off the water at the main valve if you can reach it safely.
  • Stay out of standing water near outlets, cords and appliances.
  • Photograph the damage before you move anything.
  • Have your address, cross street and a short description of the loss ready.
