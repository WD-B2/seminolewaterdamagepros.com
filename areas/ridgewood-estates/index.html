Water damage restoration in Ridgewood Estates

Ridgewood Estates is an established single-family neighborhood in ZIP code 33772, near Park Boulevard and Seminole City Center. Sources date most homes to the late 1950s and early 1960s, with listings showing builds from 1958. Streets run along the 83rd, 84th, 85th and 86th Avenue grid, with Ridgewood Groves and newer infill nearby.

Calls to the 24/7 line go to the Park Boulevard North office, which sends a crew the same day when one is free. Have the address ready and call (727) 626-0985. Name the rooms that are wet and, where you safely can, stop the water at the main.

Old homes and new homes on one grid

The neighborhood includes original houses from around 1960 and newer homes built in the 2020s on cleared lots, including the private Estates at Ridgewood community. The two groups fail in different ways, so the first question is always how old the house is.

Original homes often have older water heaters, patched supply lines and air handlers in closets or attics. Newer homes have modern plumbing, but they also have long runs of flexible lines and tightly sealed envelopes that hold moisture once it gets in. Both are slab construction, so water spreads under flooring before it shows.

Why speed matters on a slab

Water on a slab floor moves outward under carpet, cabinets and baseboards. By the time a stain appears on drywall, the pad underneath may already be soaked. Wet porous materials in Florida humidity can support mold growth within a couple of days, which is the 24 to 48 hour window the EPA describes.

Extraction comes first, then removal of anything that cannot be saved, then dehumidifiers and air movers with logged readings. Our structural drying page explains the process, and our guide on how long water damage takes to dry sets expectations.

Flood and storm questions

Flood-zone status for this neighborhood has not been verified against FEMA maps, and it can differ lot by lot. The Pinellas County flood zone lookup and the FEMA Flood Map Service Center will show your parcel. Hurricane season runs from June to November, and Pinellas County publishes evacuation zones.

If rain or backup water enters from outside, the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard usually treats it as Category 3, and porous materials normally come out. See our flood damage page.

Buying or selling in Ridgewood

With homes of several ages on one grid, buyers sometimes ask what to check for old water damage. Stains, soft baseboards and musty closets are common signs. Our guide to checking a home for water damage before buying lists what to look at.
