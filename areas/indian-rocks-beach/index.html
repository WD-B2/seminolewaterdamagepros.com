What makes barrier island water damage different

Homes on the island sit close to tidal water and often stand on pilings or raised slabs. Storm surge and rain can reach lower levels, garages and ground-floor rooms. Salt air corrodes fittings, hose bibs and water heater connections faster than it does inland, so plumbing leaks are more common in older houses and condos.

High humidity off the water means wet materials dry slowly and mold has an easy start.

The 2024 hurricane season put surge and heavy rain on Pinellas barrier communities, and many owners have since learned how their flood zone and insurance really work. Check the FEMA Flood Map Service Center for your address, and ask your insurer whether storm surge, wind-driven rain and flood are each covered.

Neighborhoods and landmarks we serve

Indian Rocks Beach is a small city, roughly a few thousand residents, and it includes the beachfront stretch along Gulf Boulevard, the older cottages near the Walsingham Road corridor and the canal-front streets on the Intracoastal side. The Indian Rocks Beach Nature Preserve and the numbered beach access points draw walkers and visitors, and a long run of condominium buildings line the Gulf.

We dispatch to all of them, as well as the businesses along Gulf Boulevard that serve visitors.

ZIP codes 33785 and 33786 cover the area. If you are on the northern end near Indian Shores or the southern end near Belleair Shores, tell the dispatcher your cross street so the crew arrives on the right side.

Condos, rentals and vacation homes

A large share of island properties are condos or short-term rentals. A burst supply line in a unit often reaches the unit below, and the association, owner and tenant all have different roles. Tell the dispatcher whether the building is managed, and take photos before cleanup.

If you are away from the island, a neighbor or property manager can let a crew in, but you should confirm access with your association.

We handle water events in occupied and vacant units, and drying logs help each party's insurer see what was done. Our guide on water damage claim steps outlines the documentation.

Island jobs vary with the water category, the floor area, the number of rooms and the drying days. Salt air and humidity can lengthen drying.
