Hardwood Floor Water Damage Repair in Seminole, FL

Wood absorbs water and swells within hours. We extract, dry and measure the floor so you know whether it can be saved or must be replaced.

What water does to a wood floor

Hardwood is hygroscopic, meaning it takes on and releases moisture with the surrounding air. When liquid water reaches it, the boards absorb it through the edges and underside, swell and push against each other. Early signs are cupping, where the edges rise above the center, and crowning, where the center rises. Severe cases show buckling, where boards lift off the subfloor entirely.

The type of floor matters. Solid hardwood can often be dried and refinished if caught early. Engineered wood has a thin top layer over plywood and is less forgiving. Laminate swells at the seams and is rarely saved once it has swollen.

What to do in the first hours

  1. Stop the source of water and mop up the surface with towels. Do not use heat guns or a hair dryer, which can cause cracking.
  2. Lift area rugs and furniture, and put foil or plastic blocks under table legs.
  3. Keep the air conditioner running, but do not point fans at the floor unless advised by the crew.
  4. Do not sand or refinish until the floor is measured as dry.
  5. Call (727) 626-0985 so a crew can extract and begin controlled drying.

The dispatch line is open 24/7. A wood floor wet for more than a day or two has a worse outlook, so call early.

How hardwood is dried

Hardwood dries slowly, and drying it too fast causes cracking. The crew uses a moisture meter calibrated for the wood species to find the wettest boards, then sets dehumidifiers and low-flow air movers. For floors with trapped moisture beneath, specialty mat systems pull moisture up through the boards by vacuum. Readings are logged each visit.

  1. Measure

    Meter readings mark the wet area and set the drying goal using dry reference boards.

  2. Extract

    Surface water is removed, and wet underlayment is pulled where it traps moisture.

  3. Dry under control

    Dehumidifiers and mats dry the boards gradually to avoid cracks.

  4. Decide

    After drying, we assess flatness. Cupped boards may flatten, and permanently damaged boards are replaced and refinished.

Wood floors in Seminole homes

Many Seminole homes have hardwood or engineered flooring installed over concrete slabs, usually with a moisture barrier or glue. In Florida's humid air, a floor can already carry elevated moisture, so a leak pushes it past its limit faster.

Open floor plans in newer subdivisions such as Seminole Ridge let water travel from a kitchen or bathroom into living areas on a continuous floor, as covered in our kitchen water damage page.

Cost factors and insurance

Cost depends on the square footage, the floor type, whether the floor can be dried or must be replaced, and how well the new boards will match. Refinishing a whole room is often needed to blend a repair.

Insurers often cover sudden water events and often exclude long-term seepage, so describe the timeline honestly. Read your policy and ask your insurer. Our post on how much water damage restoration costs covers the general price drivers.
