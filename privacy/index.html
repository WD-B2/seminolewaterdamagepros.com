Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Seminole Water Damage Pros collects when you call us or use this website.

Call (727) 626-0985

Information we collect

When you call (727) 626-0985, we may collect your name, phone number, address, a description of the water damage and details of your insurance if you choose to share them. We use this information to arrange service, give you steps while you wait and keep a record of the job.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. By continuing a call you agree to the recording.

Website information

This site has no forms and does not ask you to submit personal information. Standard server logs and hosting analytics may record your IP address, browser type and the pages you view. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and an embedded Google map, which may set their own cookies under their own policies.

How we use information

  • To answer calls, dispatch crews and complete work.
  • To document the loss for you and your insurer.
  • To improve our service and train staff.
  • To meet legal obligations.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share job information with your insurer or adjuster at your request, with subcontractors who work on your job and with service providers that help us operate, or when the law requires.

Retention and security

We keep job records as long as needed for the work, warranty, insurance and legal purposes. We use reasonable safeguards, but no system is perfectly secure.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it where the law allows. Contact us at contact@seminolewaterdamagepros.com or call (727) 626-0985.

Children

This site is for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
